Automated Reporting Pipeline

Manufacturing | Power BI & Azure Data Factory

Sample scenario. A composite engagement shape based on reporting automation work I deliver — not a specific past client. Talk to me to scope your own situation.

Technologies Used

Azure Data Factory

Orchestrating data movement from shop floor systems, ERP, and IoT sensors.

Power BI

Operational dashboards for production managers and executive KPI summaries.

Power Automate

Automated alerts and report distribution to stakeholders via email and Teams.

The Challenge

A manufacturer with multiple production lines, relying on manual data collection and Excel-based reporting for KPIs like OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness), scrap rates, and production throughput. Reports come out weekly via a dedicated analyst — often too late to act on emerging quality issues. Management needs real-time visibility into production performance.

The Approach

Build an end-to-end automated reporting pipeline. Azure Data Factory ingests data from shop floor systems, ERP, and IoT sensors on a schedule, transforms it, and loads it into a SQL-based warehouse with star schemas optimized for analytical queries. Power BI delivers real-time production monitoring. Power Automate fires alerts when KPIs cross thresholds so issues are addressed as they appear.

Typical Impact

Hours Saved Each Week

Weekly manual reporting effort goes away once the pipeline runs itself.

Real-Time Monitoring

Production managers gain live dashboards instead of weekly static Excel reports.

Proactive Alerts

Threshold alerts surface quality issues as they happen — not days later.
