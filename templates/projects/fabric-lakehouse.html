Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse

Retail Analytics | Microsoft Fabric

Sample scenario. A composite engagement shape based on Fabric Lakehouse work I deliver — not a specific past client. Talk to me to scope your own situation.

Technologies Used

Microsoft Fabric

Unified analytics platform with OneLake, Lakehouse, and integrated data pipelines.

PySpark / Notebooks

Large-scale data processing and transformation for high-volume transactional data.

Power BI

Direct Lake mode reports connected to the Lakehouse for near real-time analytics.

The Challenge

A growing multi-site retailer drowning in data silos: point-of-sale, inventory, e-commerce, and CRM systems all operating independently. The analytics team spends most of its time extracting and reconciling data instead of generating insights. They need a unified platform that can handle their scale and grow with the business.

The Approach

Implement a Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse that centralizes the relevant source systems into OneLake. PySpark notebooks handle the heavy ETL — ingestion, business rules, and curated analytical tables — following a medallion (bronze/silver/gold) architecture for data quality at each stage. Power BI on Direct Lake mode delivers near real-time dashboards for store managers and executives alike.

Typical Impact

Faster Time-to-Insight

Days of data reconciliation become hours of analysis with automated pipelines and Direct Lake reporting.

Unified Data

Previously siloed systems consolidate into one governed, scalable Lakehouse.

Self-Service Analytics

Store managers gain direct access to their performance data without IT bottlenecks.
